About

Finance background, analytics habit

I spent 15 months as a Finance and Operations officer at Meezan Bank, reconciling over 200 accounts under IFRS with zero audit exceptions across that period. Along the way I built a KPI dashboard and a compliance checklist that nobody asked for, because I kept noticing the same gaps and wanted them closed before they became a problem.

That habit, noticing a manual process and fixing it before being asked, is what carried into my MSc, where I picked up Python, SQL, and AI assisted tooling to do the same thing at a larger scale.
